# Insurance Exclusions Reference

## 1. Purpose

This reference document summarizes selected exclusions and underwriting restrictions.

This document is for training and reference purposes. In the event of any conflict, the applicable policy wording and endorsements prevail.

## 2. Commercial Property Exclusions

Commercial property coverage does not apply to:

- Flood, unless flood coverage has been added by endorsement.
- Earthquake, unless earthquake coverage has been added by endorsement.
- Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, corrosion, rust, mould, or rot.
- Repeated seepage or leakage occurring over 14 days or more.
- Intentional acts committed by or at the direction of an insured.
- Loss caused by neglect after a loss, where reasonable steps to protect property were not taken.

## 3. Cyber Insurance Underwriting Restrictions

The following exposures require referral and may be declined depending on the complete submission:

- Cryptocurrency exchanges.
- Online gambling businesses.
- Critical infrastructure operators.
- Organizations with a ransomware incident in the previous 36 months.
- Organizations without multi-factor authentication for remote access.

These underwriting restrictions are not policy exclusions.

## 4. Distinction Between Exclusions and Underwriting Rules

A policy exclusion limits or removes coverage under an issued policy.

An underwriting rule determines whether a risk may be quoted, bound, referred, or declined.

A risk that triggers an underwriting referral may still be eligible for coverage if approved by an authorized underwriter and written under applicable terms.
