# Cyber Insurance Underwriting Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide sets out the minimum information required to assess a cyber insurance submission for small and mid-sized commercial organizations.

The guide is intended for underwriting purposes only. It does not amend, extend, or restrict coverage under a cyber insurance policy.

## 2. Required Submission Information

Before an underwriter can assess a submission, the broker or applicant must provide:

- Legal name and business address of the applicant.
- Description of business operations and industry classification.
- Annual gross revenue.
- Number of employees.
- Number of records containing personal, payment-card, health, or confidential information.
- Description of the applicant's information technology environment.
- Five-year cyber incident and insurance claims history.
- Existing cyber insurance details, including limits, deductibles, and expiry date.

Incomplete submissions must not be quoted until the missing information is obtained or an underwriter approves an exception.

## 3. Minimum Security Controls

For organizations with annual revenue above CAD 5 million, multi-factor authentication must be enabled for remote access, email accounts, and privileged administrator accounts.

The applicant must maintain backups of critical systems and data. Backups must be tested at least annually.

Endpoint detection and response or equivalent endpoint protection is required for organizations with annual revenue above CAD 10 million.

## 4. Referral Criteria

The submission must be referred to a senior underwriter if any of the following applies:

- Annual revenue exceeds CAD 50 million.
- The applicant has experienced a ransomware incident in the past 36 months.
- The applicant does not use multi-factor authentication for remote access.
- The applicant operates in cryptocurrency exchange, online gambling, or critical infrastructure.
- The requested aggregate limit exceeds CAD 5 million.

## 5. Standard Deductibles

The minimum standard deductible is CAD 5,000 for organizations with annual revenue below CAD 10 million.

The minimum standard deductible is CAD 10,000 for organizations with annual revenue of CAD 10 million or more.

A deductible lower than the applicable minimum requires senior underwriting approval.

## 6. Cyber Coverage Reminder

Underwriting requirements, referral criteria, and security controls are risk-selection criteria.

They do not by themselves determine whether a specific cyber event is covered, excluded, or subject to a policy condition. Coverage must be determined by the applicable policy wording and endorsements.
