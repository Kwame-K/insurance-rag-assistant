# Commercial Property Policy

## 1. Insuring Agreement

Subject to the terms, conditions, limits, deductibles, and exclusions of this policy, the insurer will indemnify the insured for direct physical loss of or damage to covered property arising from a covered cause of loss.

Covered property includes buildings, business personal property, stock, furniture, equipment, and tenant improvements located at a declared insured location.

## 2. Deductible

The insured is responsible for the deductible shown in the schedule for each occurrence.

The standard commercial property deductible is CAD 2,500 per occurrence.

For water damage claims, the applicable deductible is CAD 5,000 per occurrence.

For losses caused by flood, the applicable deductible is CAD 10,000 per occurrence, where flood coverage has been purchased by endorsement.

## 3. Water Damage Coverage

Water damage caused by the sudden and accidental escape of water from plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or fire-protection systems is covered, subject to the applicable deductible.

Coverage does not apply to repeated seepage, leakage, or overflow occurring over a period of 14 days or more.

## 4. Flood Exclusion

This policy does not cover loss or damage caused directly or indirectly by flood.

Flood means the rising, breaking out, or overflow of any natural or artificial body of water, including rivers, lakes, streams, and surface water.

This exclusion applies whether or not another cause contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

## 5. Flood Endorsement

Flood coverage may be added by endorsement, subject to underwriting approval.

Where the Flood Endorsement has been purchased, the Flood Exclusion does not apply to covered property located at the insured location shown in the endorsement schedule.

Flood coverage under the endorsement is subject to a CAD 10,000 deductible per occurrence.

## 6. Vacancy Condition

If a building has been vacant for more than 60 consecutive days, the insurer is not liable for loss or damage caused by vandalism, malicious acts, water damage, or sprinkler leakage.

## 7. Duties Following Loss

The insured must provide prompt written notice of loss.

The insured must take reasonable steps to protect covered property from further damage and must provide records, invoices, photographs, and other documentation reasonably required by the insurer.
